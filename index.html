Meet Christine

CLINICAL
EXPERTISE.
PERSONALIZED
CARE.

Christine Klody, PT, DPT, MPT, CSCS is a physical therapist and the Founder and President of DPT Sport. Her professional work centers on customized rehabilitation, outpatient orthopaedics, sports medicine, and performance-oriented care.

Based in the Burr Ridge / Hinsdale, Illinois area, Christine is identified in public professional sources as Director of Golf Rehab & Sports Performance, reflecting her work at the intersection of clinical rehabilitation and athletic performance. She has practiced in the private-practice setting since 2003, with a primary focus on outpatient orthopaedics and sports medicine, primarily treating adults.

Professional Philosophy

THE APPROACH

The following reflects the professional philosophy that informs the DPT Sport approach to physical therapy and performance care.

01
Individualized

Care and programming should reflect the individual — their goals, movement needs, physical history, and personal circumstances.

02
Evidence-Informed

Clinical decision-making should draw from current evidence, professional expertise, and the specific needs of each individual.

03
Performance-Focused

Rehabilitation can be meaningfully connected to movement, activity, and performance goals that matter to the individual.

04
Collaborative

Clear communication and professional collaboration help create a more connected, transparent care experience.
